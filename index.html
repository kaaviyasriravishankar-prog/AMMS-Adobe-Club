01 / ABOUT THE CLUB

Good ideas don’t
make themselves.

AMMS Adobe Club is a creative community for students who want to explore digital art and design. Learn alongside other curious makers, experiment with new techniques, and build projects one step at a time.

No portfolio or prior experience needed. Bring a project idea, a question, or simply a willingness to try.

GOOD TO KNOW

A few quick
answers.

Do I need design experience?

No. Beginners are welcome, and you can choose projects that match your experience.

Do I need my own Adobe account?

Access depends on what is available through AMMS. Ask the club organizer which tools and sign-in options are available.

What should I bring?

Bring your curiosity and, if you have one, a project idea. Check project details for any materials or device requirements.

When and where does the club meet?

Meeting times and room details can change. Ask the AMMS Adobe Club organizer or check the latest club announcements.

How do I join?

Ask the club organizer at AMMS about sign-up, upcoming sessions, and any school requirements.
