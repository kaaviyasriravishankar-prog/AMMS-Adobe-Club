01 / ABOUT THE CLUB

Have an idea?
Let’s create it!

AMMS Adobe Club is a fun and creative club where students can explore Adobe tools and learn new design skills. We create projects like posters, graphics, videos, and other digital designs. Students can also take on different roles, work as a team, and help plan and create club projects. Whether you are new to Adobe or already enjoy designing, everyone can learn, create, and share their ideas. Our goal is to have fun, build creative skills, and work together!

No portfolio or prior experience needed. Bring a project idea, a question, or simply a willingness to try.
